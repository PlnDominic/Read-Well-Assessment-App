Sunny's recorded voice: one clip per line he says, named by the hash of the
exact text (src/lib/voiceKey.ts) and listed in src/lib/voiceManifest.json.

Made with a voice cloned from a consenting speaker's recording, using the open
source Chatterbox model (MIT license) on Google Colab (see voice-clone/). The
accent follows the speaker's sample. Three Level 1 story listening lines (the
Kwaku goat story and its first two questions) are still a real recording of a
Ghanaian male voice. Replace any clip with a real recording using
scripts/add-voice.py.
