The student game's sounds (tap, star, mission, quest, oops) are composed from
the sound of Ghanaian instruments by scripts/make-game-sounds.py, as plain
synthesis, so they're original to this project and carry no licence:

  talking drum  a gliding "dun" (tap, and the beat under mission, quest, oops)
  gyil          the Ghanaian xylophone, in the C major pentatonic scale (star,
                the melodies in mission and quest, and the falling pair in oops)
  gankogui      the iron double bell keeping the 12/8 time of highlife (mission, quest)
  shekere       a beaded gourd shaker (star, mission, quest)

They are inspired by those instruments; they are not recordings of Ghanaian
musicians. To use real recordings, replace the files here (same names, MP3,
mono) or ask for them to be swapped in.

(Until October 2026 these were Kenney's CC0 Interface Sounds and Music
Jingles, www.kenney.nl; they are no longer used.)
